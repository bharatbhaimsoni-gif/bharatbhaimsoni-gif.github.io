Delete your Gold Rate data

Gold Rate: Gold Price Live · by Kelmora Labs

Gold Rate has no accounts and no login. Most of what the app keeps (your city, Gold Locker, settings) stays on your phone only.

How to delete it

  1. Price alerts: in the app, open Tools › Price alerts and tap Delete on each alert. It is deleted from our server at once.
  2. Morning rate: open Settings › Notifications and turn Morning rate off.
  3. Everything on your phone: uninstall the app. This deletes everything it keeps on the phone, and our server then clears your phone's notification address.
  4. Anything else (the rest of your phone's anonymous alert record, feedback messages, crash reports and usage counts): email kelmoralabs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – Gold Rate". Because we don't know who you are, we may ask for details that help us find your phone's anonymous data. We reply within 30 days.
  5. Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google › Ads). Google keeps its own ad data under Google's privacy policy.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your alerts and morning rate settings, your phone's anonymous code and notification address, and the feedback messages, crash reports and usage counts we can link to your phone.
  • Kept: purchase records, as long as needed to restore your purchases and as the law requires. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases.
  • Deleted on their own: usage counts and crash reports, after the limited time Google and Sentry keep them, even if you don't ask.
