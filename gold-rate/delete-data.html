Delete your Gold Rate data

Gold Rate: Gold Price Live · by Kelmora Labs

Gold Rate has no accounts and no login. Most of what the app keeps (your city, Gold Locker, settings) is never sent to us.

How to delete it

  1. Everything on our server, in the app: open Settings › Delete my data and tap Delete. Your phone's code, notification address, price alerts and smart alerts are deleted from our server at once; the morning rate stops; the app's usage counts start afresh. Your Gold Locker and settings stay on your phone.
  2. One price alert: open Tools › Price alerts and tap Delete on it. It is deleted from our server at once.
  3. Usage counts: turn off Settings › Usage counts to stop them.
  4. Everything on your phone: uninstall the app. This deletes everything it keeps on the phone. Do step 1 first: after an uninstall, our server deletes your alerts only 30 days later.
  5. Feedback messages: email kelmoralabs@gmail.com with the date and the words you sent. We reply within 30 days.
  6. Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google › Ads). Google keeps its own ad data under Google's privacy policy.

Turning the morning rate off (Settings › Notifications) only stops the morning notification: the morning rate keeps no record of your phone on our server.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your alerts and smart alert settings, your phone's code and notification address, and the feedback messages you ask us to delete.
  • Not linked to you: crash reports and usage counts carry no code we can link to your phone; they are deleted automatically after the limited time Google and Sentry keep them.
  • Kept: purchase records, as long as needed to restore your purchases and as the law requires. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases.
