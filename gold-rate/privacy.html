Gold Rate privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026

This policy explains what the Gold Rate app (on Google Play as "Gold Rate: Gold Price Live") does with information. The app is made by Kelmora Labs, the developer name of Bharatbhai Mafatlal Soni, India ("we", "us"). Questions: kelmoralabs@gmail.com.

In short

  • No login. We never ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your location is used only on your phone to find your city. It is never sent to us.
  • Your Gold Locker items and photos stay on your phone.
  • Price alerts and the morning rate are kept on our server with an anonymous code, so they can ring when the app is closed. You can delete them in the app.
  • We count how the app is used and receive crash reports, to fix problems. These never include your name or contact details.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob, which may use your phone's advertising ID. Where the law asks for it, you choose first.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your information.

What stays on your phone

  • Location. Only when you tap "Use my location", the app reads your approximate location (never the precise one), finds the nearest city on its list and forgets the location. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Country. The app guesses your country from the SIM card and the phone's time zone, on the phone. No permission is needed and nothing is sent.
  • Gold Locker. Your items, bills and photos are kept only on your phone, behind your phone's lock if you choose. A backup file is made only when you ask; it is locked with your password and saved wherever you choose.
  • Settings (language, theme, city, units and so on), the home screen widget and "Hear the rate" (your phone's own voice) work on the phone.
  • Camera and photos are used only when you add a photo to the Gold Locker.

What leaves your phone, and why

Rates. The app downloads the latest rates, charts and news lists as public files from our servers (Supabase and Cloudflare). Like any website, these servers see your phone's internet address when it asks; we do not use it to identify you.

Price alerts, the morning rate and smart alerts. When you set your first alert or turn on the morning rate, the app creates an anonymous code for your phone (no account, no login) on our server (Supabase). With it we keep:

  • your phone's notification address (a push token from Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which deliver the notifications);
  • each alert: the metal, purity, weight, the city (India) or money (other countries), the target price, up or down, and its words in your language;
  • the morning rate's time and prices, your time zone, and your smart alert choices.

Only your phone can read or change these, and our server uses them only to send your notifications. Deleting an alert in the app deletes it from our server at once; turning the morning rate off stops it. If the app is removed, the notification address stops working and our server clears it.

Usage counts (Google Firebase Analytics). The app counts things like which screens are opened and which features are used, with an app-install code from Firebase, the phone's model, Android version, country and language. It does not collect the advertising ID, and never your name or contact details. We use it only to understand and improve the app. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland nothing is counted unless you allow it.

Crash reports and feedback (Sentry). When the app crashes, a report is sent with the error, the app version, the phone's model and Android version. If you send "Feedback & bug report", your message is sent with the app's language, country, theme and version. Please don't write personal details in it. We use these only to fix problems.

Ads (Google AdMob). Free users see ads. Google AdMob may use your phone's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads, and to personalise them where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first, and you can change your answer any time in Settings › Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google › Ads). People who buy "Remove ads" or Premium see no ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat). Premium and "Remove ads" are bought through Google Play. RevenueCat checks the purchase for us with an anonymous code, so the app knows what you bought and can restore it. We never see your card, bank or payment details.

News. A news story opens the publisher's own page inside the app, and the story pictures come from the publishers. The publisher sees that visit like any website visit, under its own privacy policy.

Google Play. The app uses Google Play's own boxes to ask you to rate the app and to install important updates. Google handles them under its privacy policy.

Services we use

Each follows its own privacy policy: Supabase · Cloudflare · Expo · Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play) · Sentry · RevenueCat. Their servers may be outside your country, including in the United States.

Legal basis (European Economic Area and UK)

Alerts and the morning rate: to provide what you asked for. Crash reports and security: our legitimate interest in a working, safe app. Usage counts and personalised ads: your consent, which you can take back any time.

How long we keep it

  • Alerts and morning rate settings: until you delete them, or until the notification address stops working.
  • Usage counts and crash reports: for a limited time set by Google and Sentry, then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to restore your purchases and as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete alerts and turn off notifications in the app (Settings › Notifications).
  • Change your ad choices (Settings › Privacy choices, where shown) or reset your advertising ID.
  • Location, camera and photos are asked for only when you use them; you can say no, or turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Removing the app deletes everything it keeps on your phone.
  • Depending on where you live, you may ask to see, correct or delete information about you, or object to its use. Write to kelmoralabs@gmail.com. Because we don't know who you are, we may ask for details that help us find your phone's anonymous data.

Children

Gold Rate is made for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Everything the app sends travels encrypted (HTTPS). On our server, each phone can reach only its own alerts.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. Important changes are also shown in the app.
