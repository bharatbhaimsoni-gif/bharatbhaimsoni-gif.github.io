Gold Rate privacy policy

Draft of 27 September 2026. Gold Rate is still being tested and is not on Google Play yet. The full policy will be published on this page before the app is released.

In short

  • No login. We never ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your location is used only on your phone to find your city. It is never sent to us.
  • Your Gold Locker items and photos stay on your phone. A backup file is made only when you ask for one; it is locked with your password and kept wherever you choose.
  • The app downloads the rates from our server as public files. Like any website, our server provider sees your phone's internet address when it does; we don't use it to identify you.
  • Price alerts will be kept on our server with an anonymous device code, so they can ring when the app is closed.
  • Ads will be shown by Google AdMob, which may use your phone's advertising ID. You will choose your ad settings when the app first asks.
